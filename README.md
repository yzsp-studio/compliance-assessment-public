## 🇨🇳 中文

从一句业务需求或一份 PRD 出发，梳理事实与主体关系、识别法律问题、检索公开法规与案例，产出带来源、风险等级和整改建议的合规评估报告。它先确认适用法域，再按「谁与谁形成什么关系、评估对象处于什么法律地位」组织问题；涉及数据隐私、广告营销、未成年人、竞争、知识产权、AI 内容、贸易管制等方向时，加载对应的公开领域包。工作流有两道必经的用户确认闸门：A 阶段的业务理解纪要与路线选择，以及生成报告前的生成物形式确认；即便用户授权直出，也不得跳过法理梳理与事实缺口披露。交付的是一份连贯的律师式说理报告，含结论摘要、业务背景与假设、待专业人员确认事项、可执行整改建议与责任角色、风险汇总表、免责声明与文末来源，可导出 Markdown 与 Word；不编造法规、条号、案例或链接，没有可靠依据时标记「需专业人员确认」。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 从一句业务需求或 PRD 出发，提炼运营主体、参与方、核心动作与数据／资金／内容流
- 先确认适用法域，再按法律关系组织问题清单；前提无法确定时用条件分析，而不是拍脑袋定性
- 两道硬闸门：A 阶段的「纪要＋下一步选择块」不可跳过，E 阶段前必须确认生成物形式
- 三级风险口径：禁止或重大风险／可整改风险／需专业人员确认，公开行业文章不能单独支撑定性
- 报告为连贯律师式说理，含整改建议与责任角色、风险汇总表、免责声明与文末来源，可导出 Markdown + Word
- PRD 审阅分支逐段给风险、原因、建议、待确认事实与依据，除非用户明确要求，不改写 PRD 正文

## 🇬🇧 English

Starts from a one-line business need or a PRD and produces a compliance assessment report with sources, risk grades, and remediation advice: facts and entity relationships are mapped, legal issues identified, and public laws and cases searched. It confirms the applicable jurisdiction first, then organizes issues around who forms what relationship with whom and what legal position the assessed product occupies, loading public domain packs for data privacy, advertising and marketing, minors, competition, intellectual property, AI content, trade controls, and more. Two user-confirmation gates are mandatory: the business briefing plus route choice in stage A, and the deliverable-format check before report generation; even an explicit direct-report authorization cannot skip the legal issue mapping or the disclosure of factual gaps. The deliverable is a coherent, lawyer-style reasoned report with an executive summary, background and assumptions, items needing professional confirmation, actionable remediation assigned to generic roles, a risk summary table, a disclaimer, and endnote sources, exportable as Markdown and Word. It never invents statutes, article numbers, cases, or links, and marks anything unverifiable as needing professional confirmation.

### ✨ What it does

- Turns a one-line need or PRD into a map of operators, parties, core actions, and data, money, and content flows
- Confirms jurisdiction first, then organizes the issue list by legal relationship, using conditional analysis instead of guessing when premises are unknown
- Two hard gates: the stage-A briefing plus next-step choice block, and a deliverable-format check before the report
- Three risk tiers — prohibited or major, remediable, and needs professional confirmation — with public industry articles never supporting a verdict alone
- Produces a coherent lawyer-style reasoned report with remediation, responsible roles, a risk table, a disclaimer, and endnote sources, exportable as Markdown and Word
- The PRD review branch flags paragraph-level risk, cause, advice, open facts, and basis without rewriting the PRD body unless asked
